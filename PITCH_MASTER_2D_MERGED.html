Controls & Simulation Guide

Movement & Sprint: WASD to move & aim, hold Shift to sprint.

Passing & Through Balls: J Short ground pass, E Long/Aerial pass, U Through ball into space.

Shooting & Skills: Hold Space to charge Power Shot, C Finesse Shot (near box) or Skill Feint / Burst (dribbling).

Defending & Switching: J Standing tackle / press, K Slide tackle (watch timing & angle to avoid fouls/cards!), Tab Switch player (Club Career only).

Live Tactical Mentality: Keys 1 Park Bus, 2 Balanced, 3 Attacking, 4 Gegenpress.

🏟️ Club Career Mode (Full Team Control)

Take full managerial and on-pitch control of a football club. Switch freely between all 11 footballers during matches using Tab, configure tactical formations, negotiate transfers, and lead your squad to domestic and continental glory.


⭐ Player Career Mode (Locked Single-Pro Control)

Experience football through the eyes of a single professional footballer. Player switching (Tab) is disabled during matches—you control only your footballer while AI controls your 10 teammates. Call for passes with J/U, earn match ratings, level up attributes, and accept transfer offers.
